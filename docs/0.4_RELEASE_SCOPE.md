# 0.4 — a usable planet for 0.5

Ship a stable, explorable planet that creature and cognition work can use in 0.5.

## Release requirements

- Launch the installed build on the Mac, enter the planet and leave cleanly.
- Spawn above solid ground; walk and look around with reliable collision across terrain boundaries.
- Show land and a visible, correctly placed water surface at a usable demonstration site.
- Demonstrate a falling test mass under gravity without accelerating physics for the camera.
- Show water moving downhill and accumulating in lower ground, with its inventory and transfers booked.
- Demonstrate basic wind and rain acting in the world. Verify stable atmospheric rest and transport;
  book precipitation into the same water inventory as surface flow.
- Keep input and rendering responsive during expensive planet and model work through asynchronous
  execution and results applied at simulation boundaries. LLM examples load asynchronously,
  stream genuine model output and show failures explicitly.
- Run physics in a standalone native process with its own clock and compute device; keep Godot
  responsive as an asynchronous client. Coalesce view publication before extraction, preserve events
  and trajectory history, and keep physical advancement independent of UI framerate.
- Use one authoritative representation for terrain and collision. Keep the active process set explicit;
  a process absent from the release world is absent from its controls and claims.
- Verify active paths on the available hardware and obtain acceptance of the explorable planet demo.
  Run required repository checks; name every failure and every unexamined arm.

## Planet development after this release

Advanced climate modeling, sediment-driven geology, ocean chemistry, abiotic reactions, mantle
processes and magnetism are broader planet work. They do not all
have to ship before creature work starts. Implement the acoustic, heat and phase behavior needed
for stable water flow and basic weather; comprehensive extensions keep their own requirements.

Preserve their requirements in [PHYSICS_TODO.md](PHYSICS_TODO.md), their reproduction commands in
[PLANET_BACKLOG.md](PLANET_BACKLOG.md), and their costs in [PERFORMANCE_TODO.md](PERFORMANCE_TODO.md).
A defect in a process used by the release remains a blocker. Narrow the active process set rather
than silently suppressing its errors or claiming an unfinished process is complete.

Physical laws, conservation audits and honest diagnostics still apply to active simulation.
Any new stand-in or physical approximation needs its explicit declaration and applicable approval.
This scope change authorizes postponing subsystems; it does not authorize invented physics.

0.5 prioritizes [creatures](0.5_CREATURE_FEATURES.md) and
[asynchronous runtime cognition](0.5_RUNTIME_COGNITION.md) against the accepted planet.
