# Declared approximations

Every cheaper-solver-of-the-same-shape lives here. The rule and its three conditions are in `CLAUDE.md`;
this is the scoreboard, and progress is rows leaving it as compute allows.

A row is only legal if the SHAPE is real — the functional form, the dependencies and the direction of every
effect — and only the resolution or the solver is cheapened. A number back-derived from the answer is a
fitted constant, not an approximation, and belongs in neither this file nor the tree.

Mark the site in code with `LA_APPROX: <key>` on the line above it. `scripts/check_approximations.sh` fails
the build when a marker has no row, when a row has no marker, or when a row names no replacement.

| key | stands in for | what swaps it in |
|---|---|---|
| `implicit_acoustics` | The exact Riemann face flux of the compressible equations, resolved at the sound-speed CFL. The acoustic half of the momentum equation is solved implicitly instead: each side of a face reconstructs its own state AT that face from its own hydrostatic profile (`la_stack_face_p` in `stack.glsli`, over the face's depth in that cell's own gravity), the pair states one shared face pressure and one predicted velocity per band, and the pressure correction p' that closes continuity over the step satisfies (V rho / K) p' + dt^2 div(grad p') = -dt div(m*) on the same finite-volume face graph `gravity_poisson.glsl` relaxes, with the cell's own compliance V rho / K on the diagonal — K the Reuss average of the phases standing in the cell (Wood, A Textbook of Sound, 1930). Backward Euler in the acoustic wave, so the wave is damped rather than propagated at its own speed, and the operator is linearised about the step's opening state, so a strong front's nonlinear wave fan is not resolved. Every crossing still carries the donor's face enthalpy and the interface's momentum, and the books close exactly: only the solver is cheapened, never the conservation. | An exact or HLLC Riemann face flux at the sound-speed CFL, at the same seam: `face_states` states the face and `move_momentum` closes the cell. |
| `fixed_cloud_droplet_radius` | The droplet size distribution a cloud's optical depth is an integral over. `tau = 3 LWP / (2 rho_w r_eff)` is the geometric-optics limit for droplets far larger than the wavelength, which is the real form and carries the real dependence on liquid water path and on droplet size; `r_eff` is held at the marine-stratus retrieval mean instead of coming out of a droplet population, so a cloud of few large drops is made too bright and one of many small drops too dim. | A droplet-number budget: condensation nuclei carried as a channel, `r_eff` read off the condensed water per nucleus, and the same expression evaluated on it. |
| `constant_specific_heat_per_phase` | The heat capacity of each phase against temperature. `LASubstances` carries one `c` per phase and the ladder integrates `h = c T` over each branch, so the enthalpy is exact in form and the latent steps are the measured ones, but a rock's Cp rises about a third from room temperature to its solidus and the sensible part of the ladder is that much off at magmatic temperatures. | A Cp(T) polynomial per phase (Maier-Kelley or Shomate) in `LASubstances`, integrated analytically over the same branches. |
| `six_ray_longwave_gather` | The angular quadrature of the longwave gather. A cell gathers along its own six face normals and weights each by that face's own solid-angle share, so the whole sphere is covered once and the flux divergence closes; a real quadrature takes several rays per octant, and six directions cannot resolve a gradient that is not aligned with the grid. | A discrete-ordinates set (S4 or S8) over the same per-band optical depths, once the marches per cell are affordable. |
| `march_enters_first_subface` | The cell a ray leaves through where a coarse cell meets four finer ones across one slot. The march enters the first of the four instead of the one the ray's own transverse position selects, so a column integral through a 2:1 seam follows a neighbouring column for the hops past it. The integrand, the step length and the direction are the exact ones; only which of four equal-area subfaces the ray takes is fixed. | The four subfaces' area-weighted mean behind the face, or the prefix-sum sweep along each grid line that `docs/PERFORMANCE_TODO.md` item 1a replaces both marches with. |
| `single_bounce_shortwave` | Multiple-scattering shortwave transfer. The reflected flux travels only radially upward and scatters once: what a cell above the reflector intercepts and does not keep is carried on out rather than re-reflected downward, so every joule the beam march removed either lands as heat or leaves the top for space. | A two-stream (or discrete-ordinates) shortwave solve per band over the same absorber paths and albedos. |
| `band_averaged_transmittance` | Per-band radiative transfer. The longwave march Planck-weights transmittance across all bands BEFORE carrying it cell to cell, so a long path absorbs more than it should — band averaging closes windows nature leaves open (correlated-k). | A per-band march, or a correlated-k table with sorted g-points, once six directions × bands per cell is affordable. |
| `quasi_static_solid_pressure` | The pressure of the SOLID interior. An open cell's pressure is a state function of its own matter, solved from the equation of state in `state_derive.glsl`; a solid cell's drops the dynamic term and takes the weight of the column standing over it instead, starting from the pressure of the fluid on the surface. Rock's bulk modulus makes a state-function pressure hypersensitive — one part per million of fill is ~80 kPa — so the interior cannot hold a monotone profile against ordinary transport noise. | The same `la_eos_p` the open cells already use, applied to solid cells too, once acoustic sub-stepping at the rock sound speed is affordable and the seed carries the compression exactly. The replacement is the branch beside it, not a rewrite. |
| `seed_radiative_column` | The radiative transfer that sets the seed's surface temperature. `LAColumnRadiation` runs the band table, the absorber paths, the Planck weighting and the two-stream diffusivity of `flux.glsl`'s RADIATE row over ONE hydrostatic column of forty equal-pressure layers on the gas mixture's own dry adiabat, because the grid whose extent that temperature sizes does not exist yet. The functional form and every dependency are the row's; only the geometry is one column and the vertical resolution is forty layers rather than the field's cells. | The field's own RADIATE row, once the box can be sized without a temperature — or a seed that re-solves the temperature on the grid after the first field exists. |
| `seal_time_inertia` | The body's moment of inertia, recomputed as its mass moves. `LABodyMeasure` walks the field once at the seal and the spin rate is that angular momentum over that inertia, so a body which later redistributes its mass does not change its day. The integrand is the exact one, sum of m r_perp^2; only the cadence is cheapened. | A reduce row for `m r_perp^2` on the device, evaluated on the field step like every other total. |
| `unfrozen_fringe_darcy` | The water supply to segregation ice (Walder & Hallet 1985, GSA Bull. 96:336): a Darcy flux across one cell at the regolith's UNFROZEN permeability under the premelting gradient rho_i L_f / T_m (Walder & Anderson 1985, Water Resour. Res. 21:551). The real frozen fringe's permeability collapses with undercooling, which is what puts the warm edge of the frost-cracking window near -3 C; here the warm edge is the tensile threshold alone and the cold edge is the pore water freezing out of the state. | A frozen-fringe permeability law kappa(dT) in the same Darcy term, once the fringe is resolvable. |
| `parabolic_photosynthesis_band` | The Gaussian temperature response of C3 electron transport, J(T) = J_opt exp(-((T - T_opt)/Omega)^2) (June, Evans & Farquhar 2004, Funct. Plant Biol. 31:275). `RM_OPTIMUM_BAND` carries the same optimum and the same width but falls as a parabola, so it reaches zero at T_opt +/- Omega instead of approaching it. | The exponential in the same rate model, once a record may name its falloff. |
| `optically_thin_canopy` | Beer-Lambert canopy absorption, fAPAR = 1 - exp(-k_ext LAI), inside GPP = LUE x fPAR x insolation x fAPAR. The photosynthesis record is FIRST ORDER in its biomass catalyst — the thin-canopy limit fAPAR ~ k_ext LAI — with the absorption cross-section per unit plant carbon closed against the measured global GPP over the global plant carbon stock at the planetary mean insolation, so a closed canopy never saturates. | The exponential fAPAR over a leaf-area amount derived from biomass (specific leaf area x leaf mass fraction), in the same LUE x absorbed-PAR integrand. |
| `periodic_slice_geometry` | A slice's lateral exterior: the real neighbouring terrain continues past the side faces, while the slice wraps them into a torus, so what leaves one side re-enters the other. | Captured side-face states fed as CONSTANT/REPLAY once a global run records all six faces, or the full-planet grid at the slice's resolution. |
| `coarse_exterior_slab` | The exterior state in the layer immediately outside a slice face. The capture instead averages the global field over a slab one GLOBAL cell deep beyond that face, weighted by how much of each global cell the slab covers, so a face's exterior is smeared over as much depth as the recording run can express. The region, the integrand and the outward direction are the exact ones; only the depth is the coarse grid's. A face the global grid cannot separate from another is named in the capture and refused, never copied. | A global run whose cells are smaller than the slice's own cell: the slab is then the adjacent layer itself. |
| `one_way_boundary_nesting` | Two-way exchange with the exterior: a REPLAY face feeds the recorded global state in, but the global run never feels what the slice does back. | Two-way nesting — the slice's face fluxes fed back into the coarse run — or the full-planet grid at the slice's resolution. |
| `boundary_relaxation_zone` | A radiating open boundary that lets outgoing waves leave. The Davies (1976) rim instead nudges each channel toward the driving face state over the outermost rows, quadratically weighted, with every nudge booked as boundary exchange. | A characteristic (radiation) condition on the momentum-pressure system at the open faces. |
| `ideal_solid_solution` | The mantle and crustal solid solutions — olivine (Mg,Fe)2SiO4, plagioclase (Na,Ca)(Al,Si)4O8, pyroxene (Mg,Fe)SiO3 — whose density, heat capacity and fusion vary non-ideally with composition and whose melting is a two-phase loop. Each carries its measured endmember data mixed linearly at the composition DERIVED from the element registry, and plagioclase melts across its endmember points; pyroxene carries the enstatite endmember data and its incongruent melting point as a congruent one. | Margules-type mixing models and the measured binary loops (Bowen 1913 for plagioclase), in the same table fields. |
| `pure_iron_core_alloy` | The core's Fe-Ni-S-Si alloy: its density, heat capacities, conductivity and melting curve are pure iron's measured values, while sulfur really depresses the alloy's melting point toward the Fe-FeS eutectic (~1261 K at 1 bar). The composition itself is the cited core model; only the thermophysical data are the endmember's. | The measured Fe-Ni-S phase diagram and alloy EOS in the same table fields, which is also what a liquid outer core at a real geotherm needs. |
| `slice_monopole_gravity` | The gravity of the whole declared body inside a slice: Poisson over the body's actual mass distribution. The slice uploads the spherically symmetric field of the body's own relaxed radial profile, g = -G m(<r) r_hat / r^2, so relief, the ocean and lateral density structure do not perturb g. | GravityPass over the full-planet grid — it fills the same `gravity` buffer the slice path writes. |
| `seed_water_table_equipotential` | The groundwater's free surface at the seal: the Boussinesq steady state under recharge, in which the table is a subdued replica of the topography. Here it is ONE equipotential radius, the sea shell plus a height solved from the body's declared groundwater over its hypsometry, capped at the ground where the ground falls below it. That is the no-flow hydrostatic limit of the same equation, so the level, its dependence on the water inventory and the direction of every effect are the real ones; only the lateral head gradient recharge would sustain is dropped. | The Darcy row the field already steps, driven forward from this state under the water cycle's own recharge. |
| `hydrostatic_pore_pressure` | The pore fluid pressure inside a locked cell. The Darcy head between a locked cell and any neighbour carries only the elevation term, as if the water in its pores stood at hydrostatic head (an unconfined aquifer); pressure differences drive the flow only between two open cells, whose pressure is the fluid's own. | A pore-pressure channel in locked cells, carried by the same Darcy row, so confined and over-pressured aquifers drive flow. |
| `top_cell_exobase` | Escape from a resolved thermosphere and exobase. The grid's outermost gas cell stands in for the exobase: its own temperature is the exospheric temperature, its own amounts the exobase densities, and the solved potential at its centre the escape energy — while the real exobase sits where the mean free path reaches the scale height, in a column this grid does not hold. The escaping Jeans tail carries the cell's mean enthalpy plus the tail's excess translational energy; the XUV wind reads the whole XUV flux at the top face because everything above the grid is unheld. | A grid whose top cells resolve the upper atmosphere, where the exobase location, its temperature and the diffusion-limited supply emerge from the same transport rows and this law evaluates at the cell the mean free path selects. |
| `chord_quadrature_lattice` | The terrain the field reads: the volume of rock, sea and air inside a cut cell, and the signed distance the grid grades its refinement levels by: the integral of the declared shape over the cube. Read as SUB^2 chords along the local vertical against the terrain lattice, whose spacing is the box cut into POINTS_PER_AXIS, so relief shorter than that spacing is smoothed away and the sea it holds departs from the declared hydrosphere by `docs/HYDROSPHERE_SEED_REL`. The seeded air is integrated over the same geometry: the barometric profile over the cube above the level that leaves the rock and the sea below it, exact in the cube's own section and in an exponent linear over each segment of the potential the cell's gravity states, but read along ONE vertical through the cell centre -- so the potential's transverse variation across a cell, the sagitta of the equipotential over the cube's width, is dropped and a cell many scale heights wide holds slightly too much at its edges. | A lattice built over the relief band alone at the relief's own wavelength, or the generator asked directly along each chord; for the air, the same integral over the cube's intersection with the true equipotential shells. |
| `ice_ih_only_solid` | Water's high-pressure solids. `LASubstances` carries one ice, ice Ih, so the solid-liquid boundary is the IAPWS R14-08(2011) ice Ih melting curve over its whole range, 251.165 K to the triple point. Above 208.566 MPa the stable solid is really ice III, then V and VI, and the melting temperature turns back UP with pressure; here the boundary holds at the ice Ih/III/liquid triple point instead of extrapolating a curve whose phase has ended. The equation, its coefficients and the sign of the effect over ice Ih's own range are the measured ones; only the polymorphs beyond it are absent. Ocean-floor and ice-sheet pressures are an order of magnitude inside the range. | Ice III, V and VI rows in `LASubstances` with their own melting curves, selected by pressure the way the ice Ih curve is now. |
| `newtonian_sphere_drag` | The drag coefficient of a free body ramming the medium it flies through. Newtonian impact theory (Cp = 2 sin^2 theta integrated over the forward hemisphere) gives exactly 1 for a sphere, so the ram force is 1/2 rho A v^2 with no free parameter and the energy it takes out of the body is booked back into the cell it was taken from. The real coefficient varies with Mach and Reynolds number -- about 0.47 for a sphere in subsonic continuum flow, near 0.92 in the hypersonic limit -- so a slow body is over-braked and a fast one slightly under-braked. | A measured Cd(Mach, Reynolds) table for the body's shape, read at the local sound speed and viscosity the state already publishes. |
| `site_from_settled_level` | Where a demo box is stood so it straddles a shore: the columns the water inventory actually fills, which is what the hydrosphere seed reads once it has run. The box is cut before any water is placed, so the search compares the crust's own radius against the level that inventory settles to over the body's hypsometry (`LAPlanetComposition::sea_level_m`), at a few hundred directions at seed time. It decides framing only: no cell, no channel and no rate reads it. | The hydrosphere fill's own result — the columns holding liquid with air above them — read per direction once the fill precedes the cut. |
