# Declared approximations

Every cheaper-solver-of-the-same-shape lives here. The rule and its three conditions are in `CLAUDE.md`;
this is the scoreboard, and progress is rows leaving it as compute allows.

A row is only legal if the SHAPE is real — the functional form, the dependencies and the direction of every
effect — and only the resolution or the solver is cheapened. A number back-derived from the answer is a
fitted constant, not an approximation, and belongs in neither this file nor the tree.

Mark the site in code with `LA_APPROX: <key>` on the line above it. `scripts/check_approximations.sh` fails
the build when a marker has no row, when a row has no marker, or when a row names no replacement.

| key | stands in for | what swaps it in |
|---|---|---|
| `level_seam_coarse_clock` | The flux across a face joining two temporal levels. Berger-Oliger advances the fine side every substep against a coarse neighbour whose state is interpolated in time, then corrects the coarse cell with the summed fine fluxes. Here the seam face is evaluated once per COARSE substep instead, off both cells' own states, so the pair writes one flux with one dt and the books close exactly; what is cheapened is the time resolution OF THE SEAM, not the law across it, and the interior of each level keeps its own full cadence. | Time-interpolated coarse state at the seam with a reflux correction pass, or an implicit solve that needs no level split at all. |
| `single_bounce_shortwave` | Multiple-scattering shortwave transfer. The reflected flux travels only radially upward and scatters once: what a cell above the reflector intercepts and does not keep is carried on out rather than re-reflected downward, so every joule the beam march removed either lands as heat or leaves the top for space. | A two-stream (or discrete-ordinates) shortwave solve per band over the same absorber paths and albedos. |
| `band_averaged_transmittance` | Per-band radiative transfer. The longwave march Planck-weights transmittance across all bands BEFORE carrying it cell to cell, so a long path absorbs more than it should — band averaging closes windows nature leaves open (correlated-k). | A per-band march, or a correlated-k table with sorted g-points, once six directions × bands per cell is affordable. |
| `fixed_sweep_relaxation` | The per-step gravity solve. A fixed count of red-black sweeps propagates information a few cells per solve rather than across the grid, so after a large sudden mass change the potential lags the mass. The seeding solve is converged; only the per-step one is capped. | A geometric multigrid V-cycle: same discrete operator, same fixed point, O(N) per cycle. |
| `hydrostatic_seed_relaxation` | The exact solve of the seed's hydrostatic balance. The seed asks for the pressure field that makes `transport.glsl`'s `face_p()` reconstruction agree across every shared open face at once — one equation per face against one unknown per cell, the discrete Poisson problem over that same face operator. It is relaxed by Gauss-Seidel in log pressure over the exact reconstruction, re-taken a fixed number of times and swept a fixed number of times per take, so a field that has not settled by the cap is published slightly out of balance. | A geometric multigrid V-cycle on the same operator, or a direct sparse solve of the same normal equations. |
| `quasi_static_solid_pressure` | The pressure of the SOLID interior. An open cell's pressure is a state function of its own matter, solved from the equation of state in `state_derive.glsl`; a solid cell's drops the dynamic term and takes the weight of the column standing over it instead, starting from the pressure of the fluid on the surface. Rock's bulk modulus makes a state-function pressure hypersensitive — one part per million of fill is ~80 kPa — so the interior cannot hold a monotone profile against ordinary transport noise. | The same `la_eos_p` the open cells already use, applied to solid cells too, once acoustic sub-stepping at the rock sound speed is affordable and the seed carries the compression exactly. The replacement is the branch beside it, not a rewrite. |
| `acoustic_face_pressure` | The exact Riemann face pressure of the compressible momentum flux. The PGF row takes the full linearized (acoustic) p* = (Z_R p_L + Z_L p_R + Z_L Z_R (u_L - u_R))/(Z_L + Z_R) with impedance Z = rho c of the LAYER each side presents at that face — `la_stack_layer` in `stack.glsli` picks it by the face's depth in the cell's stack, the gas at gamma*p and the condensed phases at the bulk modulus `state_derive.glsl` publishes. The velocity-jump term is the flux's acoustic dissipation; the vacuum limit is exact — Z of empty space is 0, so p* is the vacuum side's zero and gas free-expands. Linearized about the pair, so a strong shock's nonlinear wave fan is not resolved, and a wall face takes the cell's own p with no reflection term. | An exact or HLLC Riemann face flux. |
| `frozen_pair_relaxation` | The viscous momentum exchange integrated over the whole step. Each face pair relaxes toward equal velocities along the EXACT solution of the two-cell drag ODE with the coefficients (density, eddy viscosity) frozen at the step's start — unconditionally stable and exact in the vacuum limit, but a coefficient that changes inside the step is not felt until the next one. | A substepped or implicit viscous solve over the whole neighbourhood, coefficients re-evaluated as they change. |
| `exponential_pair_relaxation` | The potential-driven pair flux integrated over the substep. A face whose potential difference drives a mobility flux relaxes along the EXACT solution of that pair's own first-order ODE, so the share that crosses is 1 - exp(-k dt) with k the same mobility over the cell edge the explicit step used. Same rate constant, same direction, unconditionally stable, and it approaches the imbalance rather than passing it, where the explicit k dt overshoots and empties a thin cell at a coarse step. The rate is frozen at the substep's start. | The explicit Euler step of the same ODE, which this reduces to as dt -> 0, once the row's own state sets a fine enough temporal level for k dt << 1. Held by `scripts/check_atmosphere_at_rest.sh`. |
| `split_explicit_acoustics` | The fully coupled compressible integration of mass, momentum and the equation of state at the acoustic timescale. Between momentum substeps, `PASS_ACOUSTIC` advances the pressure alone as dp = -rho c^2 div(u*) dt — the isentropic linearization of the EOS about the step's start, with rho c^2 frozen and u* the linearized Riemann face velocity — while the matter rows move mass once per field step (WRF's split-explicit form). The pressure the substeps produce is a predictor; `state_derive.glsl` re-derives it from the matter that actually moved. | The matter rows joining the substepped group, with `la_eos_p` re-evaluated per substep. |
| `second_order_birch_murnaghan` | The third-order Birch-Murnaghan compression of each solid phase. The form, the dependencies and the direction are the real ones; `K0'`, the pressure derivative of the bulk modulus, is carried per substance and defaults to 4, the value at which the third-order term vanishes and BM3 reduces exactly to BM2. That is the standard treatment where `K0'` is unmeasured, not a fitted number, but it understates the stiffening of a phase whose real `K0'` is larger. | A measured `bulk_modulus_deriv` per phase in `LASubstances`, cited. The field is already read; only the data is missing. |
| `seal_time_inertia` | The body's moment of inertia, recomputed as its mass moves. `LABodyMeasure` walks the field once at the seal and the spin rate is that angular momentum over that inertia, so a body which later redistributes its mass does not change its day. The integrand is the exact one, sum of m r_perp^2; only the cadence is cheapened. | A reduce row for `m r_perp^2` on the device, evaluated on the field step like every other total. |
| `unfrozen_fringe_darcy` | The water supply to segregation ice (Walder & Hallet 1985, GSA Bull. 96:336): a Darcy flux across one cell at the regolith's UNFROZEN permeability under the Clapeyron pressure gradient. The real frozen fringe's permeability collapses with undercooling, which is what puts the warm edge of the frost-cracking window near -3 C; here the warm edge is the tensile threshold alone and the cold edge is the pore water freezing out of the state. | A frozen-fringe permeability law kappa(dT) in the same Darcy term, once the fringe is resolvable. |
| `optically_thin_canopy` | Beer-Lambert canopy absorption, fAPAR = 1 - exp(-k_ext LAI), inside GPP = LUE x fPAR x insolation x fAPAR. The photosynthesis record is FIRST ORDER in its biomass catalyst — the thin-canopy limit fAPAR ~ k_ext LAI — with the absorption cross-section per unit plant carbon closed against the measured global GPP over the global plant carbon stock at the planetary mean insolation, so a closed canopy never saturates. | The exponential fAPAR over a leaf-area amount derived from biomass (specific leaf area x leaf mass fraction), in the same LUE x absorbed-PAR integrand. |
| `periodic_slice_geometry` | A slice's lateral exterior: the real neighbouring terrain continues past the side faces, while the slice wraps them into a torus, so what leaves one side re-enters the other. | Captured side-face states fed as CONSTANT/REPLAY once a global run records all six faces, or the full-planet grid at the slice's resolution. |
| `coarse_exterior_slab` | The exterior state in the layer immediately outside a slice face. The capture instead averages the global field over a slab one GLOBAL cell deep beyond that face, weighted by how much of each global cell the slab covers, so a face's exterior is smeared over as much depth as the recording run can express. The region, the integrand and the outward direction are the exact ones; only the depth is the coarse grid's. A face the global grid cannot separate from another is named in the capture and refused, never copied. | A global run whose cells are smaller than the slice's own cell: the slab is then the adjacent layer itself. |
| `one_way_boundary_nesting` | Two-way exchange with the exterior: a REPLAY face feeds the recorded global state in, but the global run never feels what the slice does back. | Two-way nesting — the slice's face fluxes fed back into the coarse run — or the full-planet grid at the slice's resolution. |
| `boundary_relaxation_zone` | A radiating open boundary that lets outgoing waves leave. The Davies (1976) rim instead nudges each channel toward the driving face state over the outermost rows, quadratically weighted, with every nudge booked as boundary exchange. | A characteristic (radiation) condition on the momentum-pressure system at the open faces. |
| `ideal_solid_solution` | The mantle and crustal solid solutions — olivine (Mg,Fe)2SiO4, plagioclase (Na,Ca)(Al,Si)4O8, pyroxene (Mg,Fe)SiO3 — whose density, heat capacity and fusion vary non-ideally with composition and whose melting is a two-phase loop. Each carries its measured endmember data mixed linearly at the composition DERIVED from the element registry, and plagioclase melts across its endmember points; pyroxene carries the enstatite endmember data and its incongruent melting point as a congruent one. | Margules-type mixing models and the measured binary loops (Bowen 1913 for plagioclase), in the same table fields. |
| `pure_iron_core_alloy` | The core's Fe-Ni-S-Si alloy: its density, heat capacities, conductivity and melting curve are pure iron's measured values, while sulfur really depresses the alloy's melting point toward the Fe-FeS eutectic (~1261 K at 1 bar). The composition itself is the cited core model; only the thermophysical data are the endmember's. | The measured Fe-Ni-S phase diagram and alloy EOS in the same table fields, which is also what a liquid outer core at a real geotherm needs. |
| `slice_monopole_gravity` | The gravity of the whole declared body inside a slice: Poisson over the body's actual mass distribution. The slice writes the uniform-body monopole g = -G M(<r) r_hat / r^2 from the declared radius and rock density instead, so relief, the ocean and density structure do not perturb g. | GravityPass over the full-planet grid — it fills the same `gravity` buffer the slice path writes. |
| `top_cell_exobase` | Escape from a resolved thermosphere and exobase. The grid's outermost gas cell stands in for the exobase: its own temperature is the exospheric temperature, its own amounts the exobase densities, and the solved potential at its centre the escape energy — while the real exobase sits where the mean free path reaches the scale height, in a column this grid does not hold. The escaping Jeans tail carries the cell's mean enthalpy plus the tail's excess translational energy; the XUV wind reads the whole XUV flux at the top face because everything above the grid is unheld. | A grid whose top cells resolve the upper atmosphere, where the exobase location, its temperature and the diffusion-limited supply emerge from the same transport rows and this law evaluates at the cell the mean free path selects. |
| `shared_face_headroom` | The receiver-side limit on an inflow. What a cell can accept is its own free volume, shared among however many of its faces are donating this step; each face is given a fixed sixth of it instead, off the pre-step amount. Conservative in the right direction — the summed inflow can never pass the room that was there — and it needs no second pass, but a cell fed through one face accepts a sixth of what it could hold. | A receiver-side acceptance pass between outflow and gather: sum the intents per receiver, scale them by the room, and let the donors book the scaled figure. |
