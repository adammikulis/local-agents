# Declared approximations

Every cheaper-solver-of-the-same-shape lives here. The rule and its three conditions are in `CLAUDE.md`;
this is the scoreboard, and progress is rows leaving it as compute allows.

A row is only legal if the SHAPE is real — the functional form, the dependencies and the direction of every
effect — and only the resolution or the solver is cheapened. A number back-derived from the answer is a
fitted constant, not an approximation, and belongs in neither this file nor the tree.

Mark the site in code with `LA_APPROX: <key>` on the line above it. `scripts/check_approximations.sh` fails
the build when a marker has no row, when a row has no marker, or when a row names no replacement.

| key | stands in for | what swaps it in |
|---|---|---|
| `single_bounce_shortwave` | Multiple-scattering shortwave transfer. The reflected flux travels only radially upward and scatters once: what a cell above the reflector intercepts and does not keep is carried on out rather than re-reflected downward, so every joule the beam march removed either lands as heat or leaves the top for space. | A two-stream (or discrete-ordinates) shortwave solve per band over the same absorber paths and albedos. |
| `band_averaged_transmittance` | Per-band radiative transfer. The longwave march Planck-weights transmittance across all bands BEFORE carrying it cell to cell, so a long path absorbs more than it should — band averaging closes windows nature leaves open (correlated-k). | A per-band march, or a correlated-k table with sorted g-points, once six directions × bands per cell is affordable. |
| `fixed_sweep_relaxation` | The per-step gravity solve. A fixed count of red-black sweeps propagates information a few cells per solve rather than across the grid, so after a large sudden mass change the potential lags the mass. The seeding solve is converged; only the per-step one is capped. | A geometric multigrid V-cycle: same discrete operator, same fixed point, O(N) per cycle. |
| `quasi_static_solid_pressure` | The pressure of the SOLID interior. An open cell's pressure is a state function of its own matter, solved from the equation of state in `state_derive.glsl`; a solid cell's drops the dynamic term and takes the weight of the column standing over it instead, starting from the pressure of the fluid on the surface. Rock's bulk modulus makes a state-function pressure hypersensitive — one part per million of fill is ~80 kPa — so the interior cannot hold a monotone profile against ordinary transport noise. | The same `la_eos_p` the open cells already use, applied to solid cells too, once acoustic sub-stepping at the rock sound speed is affordable and the seed carries the compression exactly. The replacement is the branch beside it, not a rewrite. |
| `acoustic_face_pressure` | The exact Riemann face pressure of the compressible momentum flux. The PGF row takes the full linearized (acoustic) p* = (Z_R p_L + Z_L p_R + Z_L Z_R (u_L - u_R))/(Z_L + Z_R) with impedance Z = rho c and c each cell's own mixture celerity off the Wood compressibility sum in `state_derive.glsl`. The velocity-jump term is the flux's acoustic dissipation; the vacuum limit is exact — Z of empty space is 0, so p* is the vacuum side's zero and gas free-expands. Linearized about the pair, so a strong shock's nonlinear wave fan is not resolved, and a wall face takes the cell's own p with no reflection term. | An exact or HLLC Riemann face flux. |
| `frozen_pair_relaxation` | The viscous momentum exchange integrated over the whole step. Each face pair relaxes toward equal velocities along the EXACT solution of the two-cell drag ODE with the coefficients (density, eddy viscosity) frozen at the step's start — unconditionally stable and exact in the vacuum limit, but a coefficient that changes inside the step is not felt until the next one. | A substepped or implicit viscous solve over the whole neighbourhood, coefficients re-evaluated as they change. |
| `cfl_substep_budget` | Unbounded explicit substepping of the transport laws. A row runs the substeps its own stability bound asks — advective courant, diffusive 6 nu dt/L^2, acoustic c dt sqrt(3)/L, gravity-adjustment sqrt(6 g L) dt/L — at dt/N, the exact law on a finer time axis; but N is capped at `TransportPass.SUBSTEP_MAX` and the CFL inputs (peak speed, peak temperature, peak celerity, mean g) are the PREVIOUS step's drained maxima, so a spike that outruns the cap or grows faster than one step is integrated past its stability limit. The speed peak is written at the end of the momentum gathers, so its lag is the reactions between two transports, not a whole step. | An implicit or unconditionally stable momentum solve, or an in-step device-side CFL reduction with an uncapped budget. |
| `split_explicit_acoustics` | The fully coupled compressible integration of mass, momentum and the equation of state at the acoustic timescale. Between momentum substeps, `PASS_ACOUSTIC` advances the pressure alone as dp = -rho c^2 div(u*) dt — the isentropic linearization of the EOS about the step's start, with rho c^2 frozen and u* the linearized Riemann face velocity — while the matter rows move mass once per field step (WRF's split-explicit form). The pressure the substeps produce is a predictor; `state_derive.glsl` re-derives it from the matter that actually moved. | The matter rows joining the substepped group, with `la_eos_p` re-evaluated per substep. |
| `unfrozen_fringe_darcy` | The water supply to segregation ice (Walder & Hallet 1985, GSA Bull. 96:336): a Darcy flux across one cell at the regolith's UNFROZEN permeability under the Clapeyron pressure gradient. The real frozen fringe's permeability collapses with undercooling, which is what puts the warm edge of the frost-cracking window near -3 C; here the warm edge is the tensile threshold alone and the cold edge is the pore water freezing out of the state. | A frozen-fringe permeability law kappa(dT) in the same Darcy term, once the fringe is resolvable. |
| `periodic_slice_geometry` | A slice's lateral exterior: the real neighbouring terrain continues past the side faces, while the slice wraps them into a torus, so what leaves one side re-enters the other. | Captured side-face states fed as CONSTANT/REPLAY once a global run records all six faces, or the full-planet grid at the slice's resolution. |
| `one_way_boundary_nesting` | Two-way exchange with the exterior: a REPLAY face feeds the recorded global state in, but the global run never feels what the slice does back. | Two-way nesting — the slice's face fluxes fed back into the coarse run — or the full-planet grid at the slice's resolution. |
| `boundary_relaxation_zone` | A radiating open boundary that lets outgoing waves leave. The Davies (1976) rim instead nudges each channel toward the driving face state over the outermost rows, quadratically weighted, with every nudge booked as boundary exchange. | A characteristic (radiation) condition on the momentum-pressure system at the open faces. |
| `slice_monopole_gravity` | The gravity of the whole declared body inside a slice: Poisson over the body's actual mass distribution. The slice writes the uniform-body monopole g = -G M(<r) r_hat / r^2 from the declared radius and rock density instead, so relief, the ocean and density structure do not perturb g. | GravityPass over the full-planet grid — it fills the same `gravity` buffer the slice path writes. |
